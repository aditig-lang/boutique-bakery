Dark Chocolate Torte

A decadent dark chocolate torte, dense and fudgy, dusted with a fine layer of cocoa powder. Intensely rich and naturally flourless—pure chocolate indulgence.

schedule Prep 15m

people Serves 10

Ingredients

  • check
    Dark chocolate (70%+)200g, chopped
  • check
    Unsalted butter150g
  • check
    Caster sugar200g
  • check
    Large eggs4
  • check
    Cocoa powder3 tbsp, plus extra for dusting
  • check
    Vanilla extract1 tsp
  • check
    Fresh raspberriesTo garnish

Method

1

Melt

Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Melt the dark chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water, stirring until smooth. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

2

Whisk

In a large bowl, whisk together the sugar and eggs until pale and slightly thickened, about 3 minutes. Add the vanilla extract and whisk to combine.

3

Fold

Gently fold the cooled chocolate mixture into the egg mixture. Sift in the cocoa powder and fold again until just combined—do not overmix to keep the torte dense and fudgy.

4

Bake & Serve

Pour into a greased 23cm springform pan. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the edges are set but the centre has a slight wobble. Cool completely, dust generously with cocoa powder, and garnish with fresh raspberries.
