Classic Carrot Cake

A beautifully moist, rustic carrot cake with visible shreds of carrot and flecks of warm spice. Layered with a thick, velvety cream cheese frosting and decorated with crushed walnuts and delicate edible flowers.

Method

1

Mix the Wet

Preheat oven to 180°C. Whisk together the oil, brown sugar, and eggs in a large bowl until well combined and slightly emulsified. It should look glossy and smooth.

2

Fold & Add Carrots

Sift in the flour, mixed spice, and cinnamon. Fold until just combined—don't overmix. Stir in the grated carrots and any optional extras like crushed walnuts.

3

Bake

Divide the batter between two greased and lined 20cm cake tins. Bake for 45–50 minutes until risen, golden, and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean. Cool completely on a wire rack before frosting.

4

Frost & Decorate

Beat cream cheese with icing sugar until smooth and spreadable. Sandwich the cakes together with frosting, then coat the top and sides. Decorate with crushed walnuts and delicate edible flowers for a rustic artisanal finish.
