Egg-free Vegetarian Floral

Lavender Honey Cake

A sophisticated floral cake with pale golden sponge lightly soaked in honey syrup, frosted with a pale lavender-tinted buttercream. Elegant, artisanal, and deeply comforting.

schedule Prep 20m
local_fire_department Bake 40m
people Serves 8

Ingredients

  • check
    Dried culinary lavender 2 tbsp
  • check
    Whole milk 1 cup
  • check
    Honey 1/2 cup
  • check
    Greek yogurt 1 cup
  • check
    Vanilla extract 1 tsp
  • check
    Unsalted butter 1 cup, softened
  • check
    Self-rising flour 2 1/2 cups

Method

1

Infuse

Gently heat milk with the dried culinary lavender until just simmering. Remove from heat and let it steep for 30 minutes. Strain the lavender out and let the milk cool completely.

2

Cream

In a large bowl, cream together the softened unsalted butter and honey until light and fluffy. This takes about 5 minutes using an electric mixer.

3

Combine

Gradually add the Greek yogurt and vanilla extract to the creamed mixture. Then, alternate adding the self-rising flour and the cooled lavender-infused milk, beginning and ending with the flour.

4

Bake & Glaze

Pour the batter into a prepared pan and bake at 175°C (350°F) for 35–40 minutes. Once cooled, drizzle generously with warm honey and garnish with a few sprigs of dried lavender.
