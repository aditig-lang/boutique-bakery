Matcha Sponge Cake

A vibrant green matcha sponge cake roll, beautifully swirled with a light cream filling. Perfectly balanced earthy matcha with a delicate sweetness—elegant and surprisingly easy to make.

schedule Prep 20m
local_fire_department Bake 25m
people Serves 8

Ingredients

  • check
    Large eggs4
  • check
    Caster sugar100g
  • check
    Plain flour80g
  • check
    Matcha powder2 tbsp, sifted
  • check
    Double cream300ml, whipped
  • check
    Icing sugar2 tbsp, to dust

Method

1

Whisk Eggs

Preheat oven to 190°C. Line a 33x23cm Swiss roll tin with baking paper. Whisk eggs and sugar together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water for 5–7 minutes until thick and pale. Remove from heat.

2

Fold & Bake

Sift together flour and matcha powder, then gently fold into the egg mixture in three additions to keep it airy. Pour into the tin and bake for 10–12 minutes until springy to the touch.

3

Roll

Turn the sponge out onto a sheet of baking paper dusted with icing sugar. Peel off the lining paper, then roll the sponge up with the paper inside while still warm. Allow to cool completely rolled up.

4

Fill & Serve

Gently unroll the cooled sponge and spread evenly with whipped double cream. Re-roll tightly (without the paper), dust with icing sugar, and slice to serve. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
